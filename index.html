Latvian Kraft

Traditional Latvian Cold Smoked Meat

Latviešu valoda

Latvian Kraft is a small artisan smokehouse preserving and continuing the traditional Latvian craft of meat smoking.

Our products are made using slow cold-smoke methods, allowing time, smoke, and wood to naturally develop deep flavor without rush.

We use hardwood firewood and selected evergreen branches to create a rich, layered aroma and strong character — inspired by Latvian forests and nature.

We believe in simplicity, raw ingredient quality, and patience — where tradition is not just a word, but a process.
